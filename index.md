# Noor Al Salah — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 11 October 2026**

Noor Al Salah is an offline-first Islamic prayer companion app. We designed it to work entirely on your device. No account is required, no analytics are collected, and no personal data is transmitted to us or any third party for tracking or advertising purposes.

## Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. All data you create in the app stays on your device.

The only data the app uses is:

- **Location (latitude and longitude):** Used to calculate accurate prayer times and the Qibla direction for your position. Your location is obtained only when you grant Android's location permission, and it is stored only on your device in the app's local database. It is never sent to us.
- **App preferences:** Your chosen calculation method, Asr madhab, high-latitude rule, prayer time adjustments, notification settings, Hijri offset, language, and theme. These are stored on your device only.
- **Prayer tracker and Quran bookmarks:** Stored on your device only.
- **Notification and alarm permissions:** Used to schedule prayer reminders and the adhan. All alarms are scheduled locally by the Android operating system. No push notification server is involved.

## Network Connections

The app makes the following outbound network calls, all optional and only when needed:

- **Reverse geocoding:** When you add a location manually or when the app first detects your GPS position, it may query OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to look up a city name from the coordinates. Only the coordinates are sent. No identifiers, no device ID, no account information.
- **Quran audio streaming:** When you play an ayah that has not been downloaded for offline use, the audio is streamed from the Quran audio CDN. Only the request for the audio file is sent.
- **Weather (optional):** If the weather feature is used, only the coordinates are sent to fetch current conditions.

You may use the app entirely offline by skipping the weather feature, choosing a manual location, and downloading Quran audio for offline playback.

## Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. We do not sell data. We do not run advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Data Storage and Deletion

All app data is stored in the app's private sandbox on your device. You can delete all data at any time by:

- Clearing the app's data from Android Settings → Apps → Noor Al Salah → Storage → Clear data
- Uninstalling the app

No data remains on any server after uninstall.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Permissions Explained

- **Location:** For prayer times and Qibla
- **Notifications:** For prayer reminders and adhan alerts
- **Exact alarms:** To fire prayer notifications at the correct minute on Android 12+
- **Foreground service:** To play the adhan in the background
- **Vibrate:** For tasbih counter feedback
- **Internet:** For optional reverse geocoding, weather, and Quran audio streaming
